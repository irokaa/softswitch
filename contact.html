About Softswitch

Softswitch is a company specialized in the study and implementation of low-voltage and low-current networks: electrical, IT, telephone networks, surveillance, access control, alarm, and fire protection systems. We also offer remote control solutions for electrical and electronic installations, as well as commissioning and maintenance of automation systems.
