Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve clients in Hassi Messaoud and surrounding areas in Ouargla, but we also undertake projects throughout Algeria. Contact us to discuss your location and requirements.

How long does a typical installation take?

Installation times vary depending on the scope of the project. A basic smart home system might take 1-2 days, while comprehensive commercial solutions can take several weeks. We provide detailed timelines during our consultation.

Do you offer maintenance services?

Yes, we offer comprehensive maintenance and support packages for all our installations. Our team is available for regular check-ups, troubleshooting, and system updates to ensure optimal performance.

What types of payment do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including cash, bank transfers, and credit cards. We also offer flexible payment plans for larger projects to make our services more accessible.

Do you provide warranties for your products and services?

Yes, all our products come with manufacturer warranties, and our installation services are backed by a comprehensive service guarantee. We stand behind the quality of our work and are committed to customer satisfaction.
